Hi, I'm Ashii.

I'm an architect who designs brands, and the rooms they live in. About four years across architecture, interiors and UI/UX taught me that a logo is only half the story. The other half is the counter, the shopfront and the phone screen.

  • B.Arch, MBS School of Planning and Architecture
  • UI/UX, Karwaan School of Design
  • ML/AI micro-certification, Product School of Design
  • Previously at GPM Architects, Studio Notion, Studio Hadi
